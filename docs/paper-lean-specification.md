# Paper and Lean specification

This comparison is for a mathematical reader checking that each formal
statement says what the manuscript claims. It is an internal specification
audit, not human expert sign-off. The design record is
`itpplasma/navier:research/evidence/cp01-lean-statement-design.md`; the
controller amendments A1–A7 applied at the CP03c integration are: A1 the
state space is `NavierFormal.Space` (no `E` abbreviation); A2 `D₃` and `P₃`
are written through `(∇u)ᵀu`, never through `∇|u|`; A3 the viscosity `ν` is a
free parameter of every definition; A4 no Littlewood–Paley objects; A5 the
classical solution class is exactly the manuscript's notion, with the
regularity package a separate predicate; A6 the Clay target is Fefferman's
alternative (A) verbatim; A7 `hyp:critical` quantifies over all classical
solutions on `[0,T')`, `T' ≤ H`, with no maximal time. Risk labels (R-NORM,
R-JUNK, R-LAP, R-P0, R-T0, R-CRIT, R-CLASS, R-REG) are those of §6 of the
design record.

Rows are grouped by the manuscript section that owns the object. "Exact"
means the Lean statement is the manuscript's statement up to the recorded
encoding choice; every deviation is named.
